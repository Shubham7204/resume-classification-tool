# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Recruiting coordinators, talent acquisition leads, and technical hiring managers conducting high-volume campus hiring drives and technical recruitment cycles (e.g. KPMG, NCS Group). They review batches of 20 to 500 candidate resumes against strict job qualifications and academic benchmarks under tight placement schedules.

## Product Purpose
RecruiterOS automates candidate screening and shortlisting using machine-native probability distributions powered by TypeSafe AI's Jev System One model. It replaces subjective, generative LLM text hallucinations with deterministic, typed classifications (role fit, rubric depth scores, capability verification nouls) and academic benchmark verification.

## Positioning
Machine-native structured hiring decisions: typed classifications with calibrated mathematical confidence intervals, strict academic cutoff enforcement, and full machine-verifiable audit logs—delivering defensible hiring decisions that generative chat models cannot provide.

## Operating Context
Enterprise campus placement seasons, high-volume university hiring campaigns, and corporate apprentice drives. Fast multi-resume ingestion, dynamic JD criteria verification, high-density cohort comparison, and rapid interview pipeline handoff.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Multi-format Job Description ingestion (PDF flyer circular or raw pasted text)
- Dynamic Jev schema synthesis with failover and model timeouts
- Deterministic TypeSafe Jev System One model integration (`/v1/systemone`)
- Automatic academic cutoff detection & warning flags (CGPA / percentage thresholds)
- Multi-resume parallel processing and text normalization
- Weighted deterministic decision engine (role fit, domain depth, capability nouls)
- 1-click CSV export and recruiter summary clipboard copy
- Slide-over full machine log inspector with raw Jev probability distribution audit

## Brand Commitments
- Name: RecruiterOS
- Voice: Precise, disciplined, high-trust enterprise intelligence
- Visual Standard: Purposeful, high-density editorial craft; no "AI slop" gradients, floating blobs, or decorative gimmickry

## Product Principles
1. **Mathematical Determinism**: Every verdict and recommendation maps directly to typed Jev probabilities.
2. **Dense, Scannable Utility**: Maximize information efficiency so recruiters can evaluate candidates in seconds without visual clutter.
3. **Auditability First**: The underlying machine probabilities, rubric levels, and raw API responses are always accessible in one click.
4. **Zero Latency Friction**: Tactile press physics, instant search, responsive sorting, and uninterrupted review workflows.
